# Oikonomia: KEY + LOCK workshop plan

12 October 2026 · Carl

*l'arte che teniamo a casa.* A three-hour session for 8 to 10 people. Each person throws, pins and checks their own practice tablet, and goes home with both faces complete and verified.

## Run of the session

About 3 hours, with a 20-minute coffee break at the halfway point, once the key side is finished. The timings are estimates for beginners working at the pace of the slowest person; the first workshop will correct them.

| Time | Min | Segment | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | 15 | Welcome: oikonomia, key and lock, what you take home, practice only | Everyone knows what they will make |
| 0:15 | 10 | The tools: dice, number sheets, tablet. One row demonstrated | Everyone has seen a full row made |
| 0:25 | 10 | Phones: open the page, run the worked example | Every phone shows the example address |
| 0:35 | 45 | Key rows 1 to 11, all together, one row at a time | Eleven rows pinned and numbered |
| 1:20 | 10 | Check 1: type the eleven numbers; the phone redraws every row; compare with the pins | Every row matches |
| 1:30 | 10 | Row 12: seven dice, then the phone gives the last four cells and the number | Row 12 complete |
| 1:40 | 20 | **Coffee.** Tablets face down in the tray, phones locked | The key side is finished before anyone gets up |
| 2:00 | 40 | Lock rows 1 to 19, copied from the phone, each at their own pace | Nineteen rows pinned, numbered and lettered |
| 2:40 | 10 | Checks 2 and 3: even rows, and the written address against the phone | Address matches letter for letter |
| 2:50 | 15 | Close: clear the phone, what the tablet is and is not, the next steps | Phones cleared, tablets packed |

If three hours is too long, the coffee break is the natural place to split it into two sessions: the key (about 1 hour 40) and the lock (about 1 hour 15), with the rows typed into the phone again at the start of the second.

## Before the day

Everything that can go wrong without a participant present should go wrong a week before, in your own workshop.

- [ ] Build one tablet per person plus two spares: board cut to 196 × 276 mm, both faces glued, turned left to right, every cell pricked on both faces.
- [ ] Push a test pin into a spare board from each face at the closest pair of cells, and check the points do not meet.
- [ ] Make one complete tablet yourself with the worked example key, start to finish, timing each segment.
- [ ] Print and cut the dice strips and address cards; put a set of number sheets in each person's folder.
- [ ] Print a small card with the page's link as a QR code, one per table.
- [ ] Open the stand-in page on an Android phone and an iPhone, run the worked example, then switch to flight mode and run it again.
- [ ] Pack the kit per person (next section) in a tray or box, so nobody reaches across another person's table.
- [ ] Translate the facilitator's spoken steps into Italian. The tablet faces, sheets, address card and pages are already in Italian or wordless; have a local check them.

## Kit

Each person works from their own tray, so throws and sheets never mix. Quantities are for 10 people.

| Item | Each person | For 10 | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tablet, both faces glued and pricked | 1 | 12 | Two spares |
| Dice: 2 orange, 5 white, 4 black | 11 | 110 + spares | Everyone throws at once, so sets cannot be shared |
| Dice strip, rows and row 12 | 1 set | 12 sets | From key-dice-strips.pdf |
| Number sheets A to D | 1 set | 10 sets | Words are not needed on the day |
| Address card | 1 | 12 | Public; can be taken home |
| Flat drawing pins | about 250 | 2,500 | Average use is about 180; a row can need all 11 |
| Fine black pen | 1 | 12 | |
| Smartphone with the page open | their own | 2 spare devices | For anyone without one, or with a flat battery |
| Tray or shallow box | 1 | 10 | Keeps the work private and the pins contained |

For the helper: a demo set made with the worked example (tablet, dice, strip, number sheets), to show a move without looking at anyone's work. On the facilitator's table: the finished example tablet, spare pins and dice, a phone charger and power strip, and the QR link cards.

## The session step by step

Each segment has one thing the facilitator shows and one thing everyone does. Check 1 comes before row 12, because a wrong number in rows 1 to 11 would also make row 12 wrong.

1. **Welcome (15 min).** Oikonomia: running a household, set against making money for its own sake. The tablet has two faces: the key, which is secret, and the lock, which is public. Hold up the finished example. Show the five hand signals: from here on, only the facilitator speaks.
    - Practise the real habits today, even on a practice key: phone face down unless in use, no photos, never say a number aloud, work inside your own tray.
    - Say plainly that this tablet must never hold money.
2. **The tools (10 min).** Dots on top is a pin, blank on top is no pin. The colours choose the place on the number sheets: orange the sheet, white the row, black the column. Demonstrate one full row on the example tablet.
3. **Phones (10 min).** Scan the QR card and open the page. It opens in Italian on a phone set to Italian; the IT / EN switch is at the top. Press Worked example, then Calculate. Every phone must show the address starting bc1qejl9. Then press Clear.
4. **Key rows 1 to 11 (45 min).** One row at a time, all together, to the five-step rhythm in the next section. Short catch-up pauses after rows 4 and 8.
5. **Check 1 (10 min).** Type the eleven numbers into the page. It draws each row's dots from its number. Compare them with the pins, row by row. A mismatch means a wrong pin or a wrong lookup: look the pins up again on the number sheet and correct whichever is wrong.
6. **Row 12 (10 min).** Throw only the orange and white dice and pin the first seven cells. Tap the same seven dots on the page and press Calculate. Pin the four grey cells as the page shows them and write row 12's number. Then coffee (20 min): tablets face down in the tray, phones locked, nobody leaves a tablet open on the table.
7. **Lock rows 1 to 19 (40 min).** Turn the tablet over. Copy the page's nineteen lock rows: pins for the lit dots, then the two numbers, then the two letters found on the address card from those numbers. Each person goes at their own pace.
8. **Checks 2 and 3 (10 min).** Every lock row must have an even number of pins. Then read the letters down the column after bc1q and compare them with the page's address, letter by letter.
9. **Close (15 min).** Press Clear and close the page; it stores nothing. A pin can fall out, so the written numbers are the real record. Mention the next steps briefly: a real key made with LOCK and steel, and later the sealed object. Ask each person for the slowest step and one thing that confused them.

## Keeping everyone together

The key rows run in lockstep, so nobody is left alone on a hard row and nobody waits long. The lock rows are mechanical copying, so people can spread out there.

**The five-step rhythm, called aloud for every key row.** It is printed under the grid on the KEY face, with the dice colour key, so nobody needs a separate card.

1. **Lancia.** Throw all eleven dice.
2. **Metti.** Put each die in its slot on the strip, colour to colour, without turning it.
3. **Punta.** A pin in every cell whose die shows dots.
4. **Trova.** Find the cell on the number sheets: orange gives the sheet, white the row, black the column.
5. **Scrivi.** Write the four digits, then lay both hands flat on the table.

- **Hands flat means ready.** The next throw waits until every pair of hands is flat. It shows who needs help without anyone having to ask.
- **One helper for 8 to 10 people.** The facilitator calls the rhythm; the helper walks and answers raised hands. A die in the wrong slot and a lookup on the wrong sheet are the most likely slips; check 1 catches both, so the helper never needs to read a row.
- **Catch-up pauses after rows 4 and 8.** A minute to finish, check a doubtful pin, or stretch.
- **Help without looking.** The helper points at the step printed on the student's KEY face, or shows the move on their own demo set, made with the public worked example. Even on a practice key, nobody reads another person's rows.
- **Spare time.** Anyone ahead on the lock side starts checks 2 and 3, then helps with chargers and spare pins, never with someone else's tablet.

**Hand signals.** Only the facilitator speaks. Students and the helper use five signals, printed at the foot of the KEY face and shown in the welcome.

| Hands | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Both hands flat on the table | Row done, ready for the next throw |
| One open hand raised | I need the helper |
| Phone held up, screen facing down | Phone problem |
| Closed fist on the table | I found a mismatch in a check |
| Empty palm turned up | I need pins or a pen |

## What "a tablet that works" means

A tablet works when its pins, its numbers and the page all agree on both faces. Three checks prove it, and each catches a different slip.

| Check | When | What is compared | Catches | If it fails |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Rows | Before row 12 | The page's dots, drawn from each written number, against the pins | A wrong pin, a die in the wrong slot, a wrong lookup or a miswritten digit | Look the pins up again on the number sheet; correct the pin or the number, whichever is wrong |
| 2. Even rows | After the lock side | The pin count of each lock row: it must be even | A missed or extra pin on the lock side | Compare that row with the page and fix the pin |
| 3. Address | Last | bc1q plus the letters read down the column, against the page's address | A wrong number or letter on the lock side | Find the first letter that differs and recopy that row |

What no check here can prove is that the key was made privately: this is a practice key that a phone has seen. The SeedSigner check (flow B) can be done later at home as an extra, independent test.

## What can go wrong

| Problem | Likely when | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A die put in the wrong slot | Key rows, early on | Check 1 catches it; the helper re-shows the strip on the demo set |
| Lookup in the wrong sheet or column | Key rows | Say the colours aloud with the rhythm; check 1 catches it |
| The page will not load | Phones segment | Spare device; the page works offline once it has opened |
| A battery dies mid-session | Lock side | Charger at the facilitator's table; the rows can be typed in again in two minutes |
| No smartphone | Arrival | One of the two spare devices, cleared after use |
| A pin falls out or bends | Any time | Spare pins on every table; check 1 or 2 shows which cell |
| Points meet inside the board | Lock side | Should not happen with the 6 mm offset; test on a spare board beforehand |
| Someone falls far behind | Key rows | The helper sits with them for one row; the catch-up pauses absorb the rest |
| A photo of a tablet | Any time | Ask before the start: no photos of the faces, only of the people |
| Someone wants to send money to their address | Close | Say no plainly: the phone has seen the key; a real key is made with LOCK |

## Open questions

- [ ] Dice: can 10 sets of 11 coloured dice be made or bought in time, and at what cost?
- [ ] Pins: what point length do the chosen pins have, and does it suit 12 mm plywood from both faces?
- [ ] Venue: which tables, and is there room for each person to work in their own tray?
- [ ] One session of three hours, or two shorter ones?
- [ ] Who is the helper, and do they make a tablet themselves beforehand?
- [ ] Italian versions of the facilitator's spoken steps and the build notes (the rhythm is now on the KEY face).
- [ ] Is the stand-in page fast enough on an older phone? Test the Calculate step on the slowest phone you can find.
- [ ] Feedback: a short card at the close, or a conversation?

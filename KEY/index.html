KEY

The key that is cut before the lock. Dice, dots and numbers in; one address out. These are the practice tools for the paper dry run.

Practice keys only. These pages are for learning and testing the process. A phone, tablet or computer that runs them has seen the whole key, so a key entered here must never hold money. Nothing you enter is stored or sent: once a page has loaded, it works with no network.
